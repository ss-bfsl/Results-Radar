# Quarterly interface direction

## Product
Quarterly gives an individual investor a calm, contextual view of upcoming company earnings and historical reactions. The dashboard helps users prepare questions and verify information; it does not make predictions or recommend trades.

## Visual direction
- Use a warm, near-white canvas, restrained forest green accents, charcoal text, and quiet neutral borders.
- Use Manrope for prominent headings, DM Sans for interface copy, and DM Mono for tickers, dates, and compact financial labels.
- Keep data dense but scannable with clear company names, consistent row heights, and aligned metric columns.
- Use green for positive historical movement and muted terracotta for negative movement or near-term urgency. Never rely on color alone.

## Interaction principles
- Stock search should accept either company name or ticker and make add/remove actions clear.
- Persist the personal watchlist locally in the browser.
- Distinguish illustrative/demo values from live, verified market data.
- Keep the layout useful on narrow screens and preserve horizontal table scrolling where needed.
